# Code of Conduct

This project aims to be open, respectful, and constructive.

## Our Standard

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and professional
- welcome feedback and good-faith questions
- focus on the work, not personal attacks
- be considerate of different experience levels and backgrounds

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment or abusive language
- discrimination or exclusionary conduct
- doxxing or privacy violations
- repeated bad-faith disruption of discussions

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or limit participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainer through GitHub rather than escalating it in public discussion.
